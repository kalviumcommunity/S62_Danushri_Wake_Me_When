Wake Me When

Not every meeting deserves to wake you.

Wake Me When is a web app that connects to your Google Calendar (and optionally Outlook) with read-only access. It checks your upcoming events against rules you set, such as your working hours, keywords like "urgent", and whether you are a required attendee. It then shows and reminds you only about the meetings that genuinely need you, by email or browser notification.

How Wake Me When uses your Google data

We use Google Sign-In to create your account, and the Google Calendar read-only scope to read event titles, times, and attendees so we can identify important meetings and send reminders. We never modify your calendar, never sell your data, and never use it for advertising. See our Privacy Policy for full details.
