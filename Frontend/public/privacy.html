Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 5, 2026 · Last updated: November 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Wake Me When ("Wake Me When", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, stores, shares, and deletes information when you use our website and web application (the "Service"). Wake Me When is a calendar-filtering tool: it reads the events on your connected Google Calendar (and, optionally, Microsoft Outlook calendar), identifies the meetings that genuinely need you based on rules you set, and sends you reminders only for those meetings.

1. Information we collect

1.1 Information you provide

  • Account details: your name and email address. If you sign up with email and password, your password is stored only as a salted bcrypt hash; we never store it in plain text.
  • Profile photo (optional): an image you upload to your profile.
  • Preferences: your working hours, whether to include weekends, alert timing, importance keywords (for example "urgent"), and other filter settings.

1.2 Information from Google (when you sign in with Google or connect Google Calendar)

With your explicit consent on Google's consent screen, we request the following scopes:

ScopeWhat we accessWhy
openid, userinfo.email, userinfo.profile Your Google account email address, name, Google account ID, and profile picture URL. To create and sign you in to your Wake Me When account and display your name and photo in the app.
calendar.readonly Read-only access to events on your Google Calendar: event title, description, start and end time, organizer, attendee email addresses, and event ID. To determine which upcoming meetings are important to you (for example, they match your keywords, you are a required attendee, or they fall outside your working hours) and to remind you about them.

We also receive and store the OAuth access token and refresh token Google issues, so the Service can check your calendar in the background and send timely reminders without you keeping the app open. Our access is read-only. We never create, modify, or delete events in your calendar.

1.3 Information from Microsoft (optional)

If you choose to connect an Outlook / Microsoft 365 calendar, we request the openid, profile, email, offline_access, User.Read, and Calendars.Read permissions. We receive your Microsoft account email address, access and refresh tokens, and read-only calendar event data (subject, preview of the body, times, organizer and attendees). We use this data in the same way as Google Calendar data.

1.4 Information created automatically

  • Important-event records: for meetings that match your rules, we store the event title, start and end time, event ID, the reason it was flagged (for example "keyword" or "attendee"), and your status for that meeting.
  • Alert log: which reminders we have already sent (event ID, reminder interval, time sent), so we never send duplicates.
  • Browser push subscription (optional): if you enable browser notifications, we store the push endpoint and keys your browser provides.
  • Session cookie: a cookie that keeps you signed in. We do not use advertising or third-party tracking cookies.

2. How we use your information

We use the information above only to provide and improve the user-facing features of Wake Me When:

  • Signing you in and maintaining your account.
  • Analysing your upcoming calendar events against the rules you set, using keyword, attendee, and working-hours matching, to decide which meetings need you.
  • Showing your important, after-hours, and overlapping meetings on your dashboard.
  • Sending reminder emails and, if enabled, browser push notifications before important meetings.
  • Sending essential service messages, such as a welcome email.
  • Notifying the Wake Me When administrator when a new account is created (name, email address, and sign-up method only), for abuse prevention and support.

Event descriptions and attendee lists are processed in memory to make the importance decision and are not stored in our database.

3. Google API Services: Limited Use disclosure

Wake Me When's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Specifically, for data obtained through Google APIs (including Google Calendar data):

  • We use it only to provide or improve the user-facing features described in this policy.
  • We do not transfer it to others, except as necessary to provide or improve those features, to comply with applicable law, or as part of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets with notice to you.
  • We do not use or transfer it to serve advertisements, including retargeting, personalised, or interest-based advertising.
  • We do not sell it, and we do not use it to determine creditworthiness or for lending purposes.
  • No human reads it unless you give us explicit consent for a specific item (for example, to troubleshoot a support request), it is necessary for security purposes such as investigating abuse, it is necessary to comply with applicable law, or it is aggregated and anonymised for internal operations.
  • We do not use it to develop, improve, or train generalised or non-personalised artificial intelligence or machine-learning models.

4. How we share information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We share it only with the service providers we need to run the Service, and only for that purpose:

  • MongoDB Atlas: database hosting where account data, tokens, settings, and important-event records are stored.
  • Render: hosting for our backend server.
  • Netlify: hosting for our website.
  • Google (Gmail SMTP): delivery of reminder and service emails to your address.
  • Your browser's push service (for example Google, Mozilla, or Apple): delivery of browser notifications, if you enable them.

We may also disclose information if required by law, or to protect the rights, safety, or security of our users or the Service.

5. Data retention

  • Important-event records are refreshed each time your calendar is checked. Records for events that no longer appear in your calendar are deleted automatically.
  • Account data, settings, and OAuth tokens are kept while your account is active.
  • When you delete your account, we immediately delete your account record, OAuth tokens, settings, important-event records, alert log, and push subscriptions from our database. Residual copies in encrypted backups are overwritten within 30 days.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Delete your account and data: go to Profile → Delete account in the app. This permanently removes all data listed above.
  • Revoke Google access: at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. After you revoke access, we can no longer read your calendar.
  • Revoke Microsoft access: at account.live.com/consent/Manage (personal accounts) or through your organisation's administrator.
  • Turn off notifications: disable email or push alerts in the app's settings, or block notifications in your browser.
  • Access or correct your data: edit your name and preferences in the app, or contact us for a copy of your data.

Depending on where you live (for example in the EU/UK under the GDPR, or in California under the CCPA), you may have further rights to access, correct, port, or erase your data, or to object to processing. Contact us to exercise them.

7. Security

All traffic between your browser, our servers, and Google/Microsoft is encrypted with HTTPS/TLS. Passwords are hashed with bcrypt. Access to the production database is restricted. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your information.

8. Children's privacy

Wake Me When is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the "Last updated" date above. If the changes are significant, we will notify you by email or in the app before they take effect.
